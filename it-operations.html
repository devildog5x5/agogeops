IT operations

On-site review of IT operations: stable platforms, maintained runbooks, change control that is observed, and monitoring directed to the responsible owner.

Inquiries Text First Then Call: 801.319.1061

The work

We come on site and review the operation as it is actually run. The review covers the systems we can reach, the people who operate them, and the leadership that owns the decisions.

Operations, security operations, and infrastructure are treated as one practice, because a weakness in any one of them appears in the others. Security, quality, and infrastructure are examined as one system, from hardware and networks through cloud and data.

You receive a direct account of what needs attention and what must change for the operation to be sustainable. The engagement establishes procedures, ownership, and operating habits your team can maintain. Leadership and assessors receive the same account the operators use.

Where the constraint is capacity, we help you add it — by contract or by hire — so the correction is not left as a finding with no one to carry it out.

What we examine

The written account covers the parts of IT operations that have to hold after the visit.

  1. Platforms

    Whether the platforms stay stable for the work the facility actually runs.

  2. Runbooks

    Whether the runbooks are maintained, and whether operators and leadership are using the same account.

  3. Change control

    Whether change control is observed in the operation.

  4. Monitoring

    Whether monitoring is directed to the responsible owner.

How an engagement proceeds

The facility assessment is a fixed engagement. The fee is paid in full before the visit is scheduled. Implementation, contract support, and hiring that follow the findings are agreed separately.

  1. Assess

    An on-site review of systems, personnel, and leadership, with a written account of what needs attention.

  2. Plan

    A prioritized sequence to strengthen, detect, document, and test according to risk.

  3. Implement and verify

    Execute the plan, assess or test, remediate, and retest until the posture holds.

  4. Sustain

    A retainer or follow-on cycle as the environment and the threat landscape change.
