ISO readiness

ISO/IEC 27001 and related control work treated as an operating requirement: evidence that can be produced, owners who understand their duties, and gaps addressed before assessment.

The work

Compliance work without both produces records without protection. Operations, security operations, and infrastructure are treated as one practice, because a weakness in any one of them appears in the others.

The work is informed by directing technology and field teams in medical devices under FDA and ISO requirements, in energy and telecommunications networks, and in security platforms, and by service in the United States Marine Corps.

Leadership and assessors receive the same account the operators use. You receive a direct account of what needs attention and what must change for the operation to be sustainable.

The engagement establishes procedures, ownership, and operating habits your team can maintain.

How an engagement proceeds

The facility assessment is a fixed engagement. The fee is paid in full before the visit is scheduled. Implementation, contract support, and hiring that follow the findings are agreed separately.

  1. Assess

    An on-site review of systems, personnel, and leadership, with a written account of what needs attention.

  2. Plan

    A prioritized sequence to strengthen, detect, document, and test according to risk.

  3. Implement and verify

    Execute the plan, assess or test, remediate, and retest until the posture holds.

  4. Sustain

    A retainer or follow-on cycle as the environment and the threat landscape change.
