What is included

We come on site and review the operation as it is actually run. The review covers the systems we can reach, the people who operate them, and the leadership that owns the decisions.

Security, quality, and infrastructure are examined as one system, from hardware and networks through cloud and data. You receive a direct account of what needs attention and what must change for the operation to be sustainable.

ISO/IEC 27001 readiness is control work treated as an operating requirement: evidence that can be produced, owners who understand their duties, and gaps addressed before assessment.

Penetration testing is informed by adversary methods, focused on the paths that matter, and followed by remediation until findings are closed and retested.

Operations covers stable platforms, runbooks, change control, and monitoring. Infrastructure covers on-premises, hybrid, and cloud foundations with defined capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery.

How the fee works

The assessment is one fixed engagement. The fee is paid in full before the visit is scheduled. This site does not publish the amount. It is part of the reply to a consultation request.

Where the constraint is capacity, we help you add it — by contract or by hire — so the correction is not left as a finding with no one to carry it out. That follow-on work is agreed separately, as are implementation and contract support.

Agoge Ops is an on-site consulting practice owned by Robert Foster, a veteran of the United States Marine Corps. The work is informed by directing technology and field teams in medical devices under FDA and ISO requirements, in energy and telecommunications networks, and in security platforms.
