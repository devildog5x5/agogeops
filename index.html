What we do

We come on site and review the operation as it is actually run. The review covers the systems we can reach, the people who operate them, and the leadership that owns the decisions.

Security, quality, and infrastructure are examined as one system, from hardware and networks through cloud and data. Agoge Ops is owned by Robert Foster, a veteran of the United States Marine Corps. The work is informed by directing technology and field teams in medical devices under FDA and ISO requirements, in energy and telecommunications networks, and in security platforms.

You receive a direct account of what needs attention and what must change for the operation to be sustainable. Where the constraint is capacity, we help you add it — by contract or by hire — so the correction is not left as a finding with no one to carry it out.

The practice

Agoge Ops applies that standard to modern information technology. Operations, security operations, and infrastructure are treated as one practice, because a weakness in any one of them appears in the others.

Engagements are conducted with your team. The work is specific to the environment you run, and it is meant to remain after the consultants leave.

Services

One practice across the functions that keep a technology environment sound.

  1. IT operations

    Stable platforms, maintained runbooks, change control that is observed, and monitoring directed to the responsible owner.

  2. Security operations

    Detection and response that connect the security operations center, information technology, and leadership, so alerts result in decisions.

  3. Security architecture

    Identity, network, cloud, and endpoint controls designed as one posture.

  4. ISO readiness

    ISO/IEC 27001 and related control work treated as an operating requirement: evidence that can be produced, owners who understand their duties, and gaps addressed before assessment.

  5. Penetration testing

    Testing informed by adversary methods, focused on the paths that matter, followed by remediation until findings are closed and retested.

  6. Infrastructure

    On-premises, hybrid, and cloud foundations with defined capacity, resilience, backup, and recovery.

Method

Overlapping coverage, examined under real operating conditions.

Aligned disciplines

Operations without security operations leaves failures undetected. Security operations without sound infrastructure investigates symptoms. Compliance work without both produces records without protection. A finding in one area becomes a correction in the others.

Tested on your schedule

Penetration testing and tabletop exercises apply pressure deliberately, so the organization sees which controls fail, which signals arrive late, and where ownership is unclear before an incident sets the timeline.

Documented readiness

Readiness is configurations, logs, tickets, restoration tests, and retest results. We record what held, what failed, and what changed.

Work that remains

The engagement establishes procedures, ownership, and operating habits your team can maintain.

How an engagement proceeds

The facility assessment is a fixed engagement. The fee is paid in full before the visit is scheduled. See what the visit includes.

  1. Assess

    An on-site review of systems, personnel, and leadership, with a written account of what needs attention.

  2. Plan

    A prioritized sequence to strengthen, detect, document, and test according to risk.

  3. Implement and verify

    Execute the plan, assess or test, remediate, and retest until the posture holds.

  4. Sustain

    A retainer or follow-on cycle as the environment and the threat landscape change.
