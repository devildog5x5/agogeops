Questions

Plain answers about the on-site assessment, ISO/IEC 27001 readiness, penetration testing and retest, and how to ask for a consultation.

Inquiries Text: 801.319.1061

What happens on site?

We come to your facility and review the operation as it is actually run. The review covers the systems we can reach, the people who operate them, and the leadership that owns the decisions. You receive a written account of what needs attention.

What does the assessment include?

Security, quality, and infrastructure are examined as one system, from hardware and networks through cloud and data. ISO/IEC 27001 readiness is treated as an operating requirement. Penetration testing is informed by adversary methods and is followed by remediation until findings are closed and retested.

When is the fee due?

The facility assessment is a fixed engagement. The fee is paid in full before the visit is scheduled. The amount is not published on this site; it is part of the reply. Implementation, contract support, and hiring that follow the findings are agreed separately.

Who owns the practice?

Agoge Ops is owned by Robert Foster, a veteran of the United States Marine Corps. The work is informed by directing technology and field teams in medical devices under FDA and ISO requirements, in energy and telecommunications networks, and in security platforms.

Is Agoge Ops a software product?

Agoge Ops is an on-site consulting practice. Engagements are conducted with your team. The work is specific to the environment you run, and it is meant to remain after the consultants leave.
